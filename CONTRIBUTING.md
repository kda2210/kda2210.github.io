# Contributing

I develop **kda2210.github.io** on my own and am not accepting code contributions (pull requests) at the moment. Bug reports, ideas and translation suggestions are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/kda2210/kda2210.github.io/issues).

By sending anything to the project (code, translations, texts) you agree that it may be used in the project under its license, GPL-3.0-or-later, and you confirm that you have the right to share it. GitHub's Terms of Service treat contributions to a licensed repository the same way. I may decline, rewrite or remove anything you send.

If you want to build on the project, fork it: see [Want to build your own?](README.md#en-fork). Please read the [Trademark Policy](TRADEMARKS.md) first.

For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.
